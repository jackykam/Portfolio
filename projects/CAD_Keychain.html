CAD Keychain
OVERVIEW

In our first year engineering studies, our introduction to Computer Aided Design (CAD) was a competition among first-year engineering students to design a small keychain. I designed a rectangular fidget toy in Onshape, which comprised of two clamshell halves housing rotatable gears.

SKILLS USED
Onshape 3D-Printing (FDM, SLA)
DESIGN PROCESS

Modeling

This was my first experience designing a printable part in a proper CAD program, and required that I learn the conventions of proper design etiquette.

  • Designing for manufacturing meant considering the orientation each component could be printed in, ensuring there were no infeasible geometries or overhangs.
  • Designing for assembly meant adding locating features for alignment and sufficient spacing for movement.
  • Finally, understanding the material from for the part (ABS plastic) determined the thicknesses and allowable tolerances of the parts.

RESULTS

The competition winners were determined via voting among all first year engineering students. To my surprise, my model was voted for top 10 designs amongst my 1000+ peers.
