Heya!

I am an Integrated Engineering graduate from the University of British Columbia. The combination of my degree, the unique capstone opportunities given to IGENs, and my own personal interests have given me a skillset to tackle complex tasks and thrive in a multi-disciplinary environment.

My drive for engineering, like many, stemmed from the enjoyment of taking things apart. However much to my dismay, an issue arose when it meant putting things back together. From this I've developed a knack for tinkering, experimenting, and breaking things in a (somewhat) methodical manner.

When not grinding IRL skillpoints to reinvest into my technical repertoire, I enjoy bouldering, swimming, video games, and working on silly little projects.

Check out some of my projects to get a gauge on my style, or feel free to contact me and say hi!
